📚 Daily Vocabulary

put up with
v. 忍受
Examples:
  - I can't put up with his behavior any longer.
    我再也无法忍受他的行为了。
  - She had to put up with a lot of stress at work.
    她不得不忍受工作中的巨大压力。

tolerate
v. 容忍
Examples:
  - We cannot tolerate discrimination in any form.
    我们不能容忍任何形式的歧视。
  - He tolerated the noise because he was used to it.
    他容忍了噪音，因为他已经习惯了。

stir
v. 搅拌
n. 搅动
Examples:
  - Stir the soup gently to mix the ingredients.
    轻轻搅拌汤以混合配料。
  - There was a stir in the audience when the announcement was made.
    当宣布消息时，观众中引起了一阵骚动。

toothpaste
n. 牙膏
Examples:
  - I need to buy a new tube of toothpaste.
    我需要买一管新的牙膏。
  - Use a small amount of toothpaste when brushing your teeth.
    刷牙时使用少量牙膏。
